For each criterion say which writeup serves that reader better (`1`, `2` or `tie`):

- `orientation`: how quickly the reader learns exactly what is proved and how the argument is organised
- `checkability`: whether each step states what it claims and what justifies it, so the reader can verify it without reconstructing missing reasoning
- `notation`: symbols defined before use, one meaning each, statements unambiguous
- `economy`: the reader's hour is not wasted on padding, repetition or detours

Then give `overall` (`1` or `2`, no ties): the writeup you would hand that reader. Give `margin`: 1 = slight, 2 = clear, 3 = decisive. Give a two-sentence `reason`.
